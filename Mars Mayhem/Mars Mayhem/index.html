MARS MAYHEM

SURVIVE MARS. BUILD YOUR FUEL. FIND YOUR WAY HOME.

You are the astronaut on the ground. Keep four people alive on Mars, then make the one thing that gets them off the planet: propellant. Power, water, oxygen and food are the daily fight — the FUEL-PROCESSOR, the rover runs and your own rest are the way home.

Mission

  • Hold HAB-Alpha together for 10 sols (~30 min at 1×) — surviving is not winning.
  • Complete 3 research topics, including the compulsory Martian Fuel Production Technology.
  • Water is finite: ROVER-01 hauls ice to AQUA-Core, and the plant only melts what it is given.
  • Collect carbon, oxygen and hydrogen — prospected at 40% — and manufacture the M3 load.
  • Fuel the return rocket at the LAUNCH-SITE and launch with all four aboard.
  • Rest the crew: every astronaut needs 4 hours in a bunk per sol, one at a time, and you cover the gap.

Core controls

  • WASD walk · Shift sprint
  • Mouse drag orbit · Wheel zoom
  • E interact / wake · C crew · R research
  • M telemetry · T time speed · Esc pause
